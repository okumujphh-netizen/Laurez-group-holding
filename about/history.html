OUR JOURNEY

24 Years of Growth

From a focused engineering and supply operation to a diversified African group with businesses spanning infrastructure, technology, logistics, real estate and hospitality.

OUR HISTORY

Built One Chapter at a Time

Laurez Group Holdings has developed through deliberate expansion, strategic investment and the continued strengthening of its technical and operational capabilities.

Each stage of our journey introduced new capabilities, businesses, assets and opportunities while building on the foundations established in earlier years.

24 YEARS

Our Journey

Key milestones that shaped Laurez Group Holdings into the diversified organisation it is today.

YEAR 10

A New Foundation

The Group entered a major stage of expansion with a stronger operating structure and increased investment capacity.

  • Profit reached approximately KSh 210 million.
  • Cash position reached approximately KSh 480 million.
  • Supply operations expanded.
  • Technical division was launched.
  • The Group moved its main office to Westlands.
YEAR 11

Heavy Engineering

Laurez expanded into heavy engineering installation and strengthened its physical operating infrastructure.

  • Heavy Engineering Installation division launched.
  • Five-acre warehouse facility established.
  • Workforce expanded significantly.
YEAR 12

Engineering & Consultancy

The Group broadened its engineering capabilities and established a stronger professional consultancy presence.

  • Road & Engineering division launched.
  • Consultancy operations expanded.
  • Consultancy office moved to Upper Hill.
  • Floors 7–9 were acquired for the consultancy operation.
YEAR 13

Energy, Plumbing & ICT

New technical service capabilities were introduced as the Group continued diversifying.

  • Solar Installation service launched.
  • Ngong Road operations established.
  • Plumbing division introduced.
  • ICT Installation division introduced.
  • Fleet capacity expanded.
YEAR 14

Entering Hospitality

The Group entered the hospitality sector through the development of a major resort project in Kisumu.

  • Forty-acre Kisumu resort project commenced.
  • Development planned in multiple phases.
  • Construction programme projected over approximately three and a half years.
YEARS 15–17

Building the Resort

The Group concentrated significant resources on completing the Kisumu hospitality development.

  • Resort construction continued through multiple phases.
  • Financing and investment structures were strengthened.
  • Shareholders became increasingly involved in the Group's expansion.
  • The Kisumu resort opened in November of Year 17.
  • Hospitality operations moved to the Upper Hill office complex.
YEAR 18

Real Estate Expansion

Real estate became a major strategic growth area for the Group.

  • Investment entered Kilimani.
  • Eight-floor development established.
  • Ten-floor Kileleshwa development added.
  • Six-floor Ngong Road block acquired.
  • Kisumu consultancy office established with approximately 40 staff.
YEAR 19

Strategic Investments

Laurez continued expanding its commercial footprint while pursuing strategic investments.

  • Restaurant launched on Mombasa Road.
  • Strategic investment in Airtel Kenya established.
  • Major skyscraper construction contract secured.
  • Contract value reached approximately KSh 3.5 billion.
YEAR 20

Logistics Takes Shape

The Group strengthened its transport and logistics capabilities.

  • Nightclub launched in Kilimani.
  • Logistics division established.
  • Fifteen Mercedes tractor units acquired.
  • Ten-acre Athi River warehouse established.
YEAR 21

Transport & Hospitality Growth

Transport operations and hospitality assets expanded significantly.

  • Nakuru resort opened in June.
  • Kilimani apartment development opened in January.
  • Ten Scania buses acquired.
  • Seven coach routes introduced.
YEAR 22

Integrated Operations

Logistics, transport, hospitality and commercial operations became increasingly integrated.

  • Additional Scania buses acquired.
  • Coach network consolidated around five active routes.
  • Three Mercedes buses added.
  • Ten-acre Kabete garage established.
  • Athi River logistics campus expanded.
  • Cold-chain logistics operations established.
  • Kiambu Road bar and restaurant opened.
  • K-Trans matatu operations developed.
YEAR 23

Consolidation & Delivery

Major projects moved toward completion while the Group consolidated its growing portfolio of businesses and assets.

  • Major construction projects progressed toward completion.
  • Nakuru resort operations continued to mature.
  • Kilimani apartment development continued operations.
  • Group businesses strengthened their operational integration.
YEAR 24
CURRENT CHAPTER

Building the Next Generation

Year 24 marks a new phase of large-scale development, strategic investment and portfolio expansion for Laurez Group Holdings.

  • Construction of the 20-acre Karen Mall commenced in May.
  • The development includes IMAX screens and major food court facilities.
  • The project is expected to develop over approximately four and a half years.
  • Strategic banking investment partnership established with KCB.
  • The Group continues strengthening engineering, logistics, real estate, hospitality and technology operations.
OUR LEGACY

Looking Beyond Year 24

The next chapter of Laurez Group Holdings is focused on disciplined expansion, stronger businesses, strategic partnerships and sustainable long-term value creation.
