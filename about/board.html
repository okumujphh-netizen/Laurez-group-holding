Board of Directors

Strategic oversight, accountability and responsible governance at the highest level of Laurez Group Holdings.

THE BOARD

Strategic Oversight for Long-Term Growth

The Board of Directors provides strategic oversight of Laurez Group Holdings and ensures that the Group's businesses are managed in accordance with its long-term objectives.

The Board works with executive leadership to oversee performance, investment, governance, risk management and sustainable growth.

BOARD MEMBERS

Board Leadership

The Board brings together strategic, financial, technical and business experience to guide the Group.

CHAIRMAN

Board Chairman

Leads the Board and provides strategic oversight of the Group's long-term direction.

EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

Group Director

Provides executive leadership and coordinates the Group's strategic priorities.

FINANCE

Finance Director

Oversees financial strategy, capital planning, reporting and financial controls.

ENGINEERING

Engineering Director

Provides technical leadership across engineering and infrastructure operations.

INVESTMENTS

Investment Director

Oversees strategic investments, partnerships and portfolio development.

NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

Non-Executive Director

Provides independent perspective, challenge and oversight to the Board.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What the Board Oversees

The Board maintains oversight across the areas that are critical to the Group's long-term success.

01

Strategy

Approving and monitoring the Group's long-term strategy and business objectives.

02

Financial Oversight

Reviewing financial performance, capital allocation and major investments.

03

Risk Management

Identifying and overseeing material risks affecting the Group and its businesses.

04

Governance

Maintaining strong governance standards, accountability and ethical conduct.

05

Major Projects

Providing oversight of major developments, acquisitions and strategic projects.

06

Sustainability

Ensuring environmental and social considerations are incorporated into long-term decision-making.

BOARD STRUCTURE

Board Committees

Specialist committees support the Board in carrying out its responsibilities effectively.

Audit & Risk Committee

Oversees financial reporting, internal controls, audit matters and significant business risks.

Investment Committee

Reviews major investments, acquisitions, partnerships and capital allocation opportunities.

Governance & Nominations Committee

Supports Board effectiveness, governance standards, succession planning and leadership appointments.

Sustainability Committee

Oversees sustainability priorities, environmental responsibility and community impact.
