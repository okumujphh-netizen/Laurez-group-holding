PORTFOLIO STRATEGY

Diversification With Purpose

The Group's diversified structure allows Laurez to participate across different sectors while maintaining a long-term investment perspective.

Complementary businesses can share expertise, infrastructure, resources and relationships while maintaining their individual operating focus.

This approach is designed to strengthen resilience, create new opportunities and support sustainable growth.

YEAR 24

A Mature and Diversified Group

By Year 24, Laurez Group Holdings has developed a broad portfolio spanning professional services, engineering, infrastructure, technology, logistics, hospitality, real estate and strategic services.

The Group's businesses provide a platform for continued expansion while supporting the long-term development of its assets, people and capabilities.
